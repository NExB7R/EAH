# EAH — Brand Brief v0.1.0

## Brand name
EAH

## Brand role
A local discovery platform that helps people quickly discover what is nearby, what fits them, and how to connect with the place.

## Brand character
- Fast
- Friendly
- Egyptian/local in tone
- Smart without feeling robotic
- Curious
- Modern
- Useful before clever

## Naming rule
EAH is the master brand. It must not be locked to food. Food can be a launch category; supermarkets, pharmacies, delivery offices and future local services live under the same brand.

## Working tagline direction
Primary working line: "إيه محتاج؟"

Secondary campaign line for food: "ناكل إيه؟"

These are working copy lines, not final legal/tagline lockups.

## Product language
- Home: "اختياراتك"
- Personalized recommendations: "على ذوقك"
- Suggestion CTA: "اقترحلي"
- Trending offers: "ترند دلوقتي"
- Nearby services: "قريب منك"
- Favorites: "مفضلاتك"
- Exclusions: "المستبعدين"
- Monthly care message: "على ذوقك"

## Visual direction
- Default theme: Dark mode
- Accent: energetic warm accent reserved for actions, trends, offers and brand moments
- Surfaces: near-black charcoal, not absolute black everywhere
- Text: high-contrast warm-neutral white and muted secondary text
- Cards: deep charcoal with restrained elevation and clear image hierarchy
- Avoid visual clutter, excessive gradients, glassmorphism and noisy shadows
- Mobile-first, thumb-friendly, fast-loading

## Logo direction
Wordmark-first. The exclamation mark is part of the brand signature and should remain recognizable at small sizes. Explore a compact app icon built from the EAH wordmark or a custom E/!/speech-bubble abstraction without copying existing brands.

## Typography
Primary UI direction: a highly legible Arabic sans for Arabic UI plus a clean Latin sans for English. Font selection is implementation work and must consider free licensing, performance and Arabic quality.

## Accessibility
- Keep contrast accessible in both themes.
- Never use color alone to communicate state.
- Focus states must be visible.
- Interactive targets should be touch-friendly.

## Social/share direction
Every listing and offer gets a distinct share preview using title, image, area and concise value proposition. Facebook/WhatsApp/other sharing must resolve to canonical public URLs.
